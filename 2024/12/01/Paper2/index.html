OsTAG: A Novel Inductor_free LoRa Backscatter Design

2024-12-01
paper

May. 2024 – Dec. 2024

Contributions

  1. Research on improving the efficiency of LoRa backscatter communication by eliminating inductor-based impedance load arrays.
  2. Proposed “OsTAG,” a novel design that virtually creates oversampled impedance, reducing chip area by 72%.
  3. Developed prototypes and integrated circuits (ICs) to validate the design’s performance and efficiency.

Key Innovations:

  1. Virtual Oversampled Impedance: Eliminated the need for large-sized inductors in the impedance load array, reducing chip area significantly.
  2. Technical Challenges Overcome: Managed oversampling clock defects and mitigated approximation errors to maintain quantization performance.

Experimental Results:

  1. Area Reduction: OsTAG’s area is only 28% of existing chip designs.
  2. Cost Efficiency: Reduced production costs by optimizing the design for mass production.

Applications:

  1. IoT Communication: OsTAG’s ultra-low power consumption (tens of microwatts) makes it ideal for large-scale IoT applications.
  2. Integrated Circuits (ICs): The design’s compact size and efficiency are suitable for integration into modern ICs.

Future Work:

  1. Scalability: Further optimize the design for even smaller chip areas and lower power consumption.
  2. Real-world Deployment: Test OsTAG in various IoT scenarios to validate its robustness and reliability.
