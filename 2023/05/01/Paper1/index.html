Design of Backscatter Tag Supporting OFDM Signal Generation

2023-05-01
paper

June. 2022 – May. 2023

Contributions

  1. Designed a backscatter communication label capable of generating OFDM signals by modulating the reflection coefficient through transistor bias voltage control.
  2. Proposed a novel approach where the label receives a pure carrier wave and reflects OFDM signals, eliminating the need for dual-channel usage and reducing requirements for the carrier transmitter.
  3. Highlighted the advantages of OFDM, including improved spectrum utilization and interference resistance in backscatter communication.

Key Innovations:

  1. OFDM Modulation in Backscatter: Enabled OFDM signal generation directly from a pure carrier wave, simplifying the system design.
  2. Single-Channel Operation: Avoided the need for dual-channel usage, reducing complexity and cost.

Experimental Results:

  1. Efficiency: Demonstrated the ability to generate OFDM signals with minimal power consumption.
  2. Flexibility: Showed compatibility with existing IoT communication frameworks.

Applications:

  1. Spectrum-Efficient IoT: Ideal for IoT applications requiring high spectrum efficiency and low interference.
  2. Low-Cost Devices: Suitable for cost-sensitive IoT deployments due to simplified hardware requirements.

Future Work:

  1. Optimization: Further enhance the label design for higher data rates and broader frequency ranges.
  2. Integration: Test the label in real-world IoT systems to evaluate its performance and scalability.
