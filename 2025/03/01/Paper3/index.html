TransComm: A New Passive IoT Communication Paradigm

2025-03-01
paper

June. 2023 – now

Contributions

  1. Introduced “TransComm,” a novel passive IoT communication paradigm that directly manipulates Tx-to-Rx signals instead of radiating energy like backscatter.
  2. Demonstrated through theoretical analysis and modeling that TransComm eliminates cascaded path loss, achieving 16.3 dB lower theoretical path loss compared to backscatter.
  3. Implemented ultra-low-power TransComm tags using low-power metamaterials, consuming only 5.3 uW per meta-atom when modulating 1 Mbps 802.11b signals.

Key Innovations:

  1. Direct Signal Manipulation: Avoids the cascaded path loss inherent in backscatter communication, significantly improving range and efficiency.
  2. Low-Power Metamaterials: Enables ultra-low-power operation, making TransComm suitable for energy-constrained IoT devices.

Experimental Results:

  1. Range Improvement: Achieved a full-coverage range of 80 meters, 8 times greater than traditional backscatter communication.
  2. Power Efficiency: Demonstrated energy consumption as low as 5.3 uW per meta-atom, ensuring long-term operation in passive IoT scenarios.

Applications:

  1. Long-Range IoT: TransComm’s extended range makes it ideal for applications requiring wide-area coverage.
  2. Energy-Efficient Devices: The ultra-low-power design is suitable for battery-less or energy-harvesting IoT devices.

Future Work:

  1. Optimization: Further refine the metamaterial design to reduce power consumption and improve signal integrity.
  2. Field Testing: Deploy TransComm in real-world IoT networks to evaluate its performance under diverse conditions.
